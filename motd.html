Build Mode & PvP

Build Mode: The default mode when you join. Build and experiment without taking damage from other players. Environmental and self-inflicted damage can still hurt you.

PvP Mode: Use !pvp to fight other PvP players. PvP remains enabled after death, but resets to Build Mode when you reconnect.

You must wait 10 seconds after dealing or receiving combat damage before switching out of PvP. PvP players also cannot use noclip.

The Basics

Build whatever you want. Wiremod machines, vehicles, structures, elaborate weapon systems, or complete nonsense are welcome.

Keep the chaos consensual. Don't grief other people's builds or deliberately disrupt players who just want to build.

Need help or want to report a problem? Contact staff or join the Discord community.
